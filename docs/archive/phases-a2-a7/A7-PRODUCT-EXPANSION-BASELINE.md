## 1. Purpose and boundary

A7T01 establishes the current repository baseline for the first bounded product-expansion capability. It inventories the existing product-adjacent surfaces (existing `virtual-account` module, `CustomerPreference` notification intent, partner/scheme metadata, callback contract, settlement contract, suspense contract, public/support/reporting surfaces) and selects one bounded first product — provider-backed virtual account — without treating existing metadata or routes as A7-approved behavior.

A7 is an Architecture phase. A7T01 is documentation-only. It is not a product activation, a partner activation, a route exposure, a notification dispatch, a public surface, or a release-gate decision. A7T01 records:

- the existing product-adjacent module and route inventory (virtual-account, customer-preference, payment, deposit, withdrawal, wallet, ledger, partner, reconciliation, support);
- the product candidate matrix (direction, capability, currency, partner dependency, provider reference, callback, settlement, suspense, notification channel, public/support surface, prohibited adjacent products, rollback assumptions);
- one selected A7 first product (provider-backed virtual account) with explicit internal command owner, partner dependency, currency, accounting unit, data fields, and prohibited adjacent products;
- the existing product-adjacent gap register, including notification delivery, public surface, and product preference enforcement;
- the product identifier, credential, secret, data-sharing, consent, retention, and legal-hold inventory;
- the A7 dependency, risk, certification, stop-condition, product-rollback, and internal-history-preservation register; and
- the compatibility classification for the existing `virtual-account` module, `CustomerPreference.notifications`, and A6 partner/scheme metadata.

A7T01 is deliberately bounded to one first product: provider-backed virtual account. A7T01 does not select a second product, a second partner, a notification channel, a public surface, a pricing model, a fees model, an FX capability, savings interest, lending, or any other product expansion. Each additional product is a separate A7 cycle and is out of scope for A7T01.

### 4.3 Selection constraints (re-stated for A7T01)

The selected first product is constrained to:

- `NGN` only for the initial A7 planning boundary.
- The A6 `NIBSS_NIP` partner boundary only; no direct calls from `VirtualAccountService`, `CustomerPreferenceService`, Wallet, Ledger, A5, or reconciliation code.
- One external bank-account inbound direction. The product does not implement outbound settlement, virtual-account-to-virtual-account transfers, internal transfers, or interest accrual.
- One explicit, verified, customer-owned external target per operation, mapped through the existing A6T04 funding-target contract.
- One existing internal customer `WalletAccount` / `LedgerAccount` source chain resolved through A3 binding.
- Positive integer minor-unit amounts and explicit `CUSTOMER_FUNDS` accounting-unit handling.

## 7. Implementation assumptions, exclusions, prohibited edges, rollback, and stop conditions

### 7.1 Implementation assumptions

- The A6 partner-adapter boundary (per ADR-0047), the A6T03 isolation/credential boundary (per ADR-0048), the A6T04 funding-instrument consumer (per ADR-0051), the A6T05 external-operation identity (per ADR-0049), the A6T06 callback authenticity/replay boundary (per ADR-0049), the A6T07 lifecycle, the A6T08 settlement/suspense/compensating-entry boundary (per ADR-0050), the A6T09 independent reconciliation (per ADR-0053), and the A6T10 data-classification matrix (per ADR-0052) remain the A6 authority boundary and are not duplicated or replaced by A7.
- `CustomerPreference.notifications` (`email`, `sms`, `push`, `inApp`) is the customer intent authority for any A7T06 delivery. A7T01 does not modify the preference entity, history, service, or controller.
- The existing `virtual-account` module is a compatibility input only. A7T02 will define the A7 product catalog and product-boundary contract; A7T04 will define the product customer-binding. A7T01 does not modify the existing module.
- The first product is bounded to virtual account assignment and inbound funding under the A6 partner boundary. It does not include outbound settlement, virtual-account-to-virtual-account transfer, fees, pricing, interest, lending, or any A7 §5 non-goal.
- A7T01 introduces no new A7 runtime code, no new entity, no new migration, no new repository, no new service, no new controller, no new module, no new API, no new route, no new scheduler, no new test, no new product implementation, no new notification infrastructure, no pricing, no fee configuration, no commission logic, no settlement logic, no reconciliation logic, and no A8 work.

### 7.2 Explicit exclusions

A7T01 does not:

- activate the first product, call a partner for a new product flow, create a second product, create a new partner, expose a public surface, dispatch a notification, or begin a notification dispatcher implementation;
- introduce a new entity, migration, repository, service, controller, module, API, route, or scheduler;
- mutate `Customer`, `CustomerWallet`, customer funding-instrument/beneficiary ownership, A3 bindings, A4 policy/source, A5 transfer history, Wallet balances, Ledger journals/lines, audit, idempotency, outbox, reconciliation, `CustomerPreference`, or `virtual-account` records;
- introduce AML, sanctions, fraud, PEP, transaction-monitoring, automated screening, or risk-scoring behavior;
- add bills/airtime, QR/merchant, agent/assisted, card, bulk/payroll, savings, credit, FX, fees, commissions, product pricing, marketing consent, or other product expansion;
- broaden the selected first product, the A6 partner dependency, or the customer cohort;
- claim any A2, A3, A4, A5, A6, Finance, Security, Privacy, Legal, Risk, Compliance, Operations, Reconciliation, Support, or partner owner approval;
- claim any A6 release-gate approval, live migration execution, partner certification, or production activation;
- begin A8, public APIs, mobile/web channels, marketing consent, broad customer activation, product expansion beyond the first selected product, or service extraction.

### 7.3 Prohibited edges

- A7 treats a product event, partner response, callback, statement, provider reference, preference value, notification delivery record, or outbox fact as canonical Customer identity, A2 authorization, A3 binding, A4 policy, or Ledger truth.
- A7 selects a `WalletAccount` / `LedgerAccount` from a customer reference, virtual-account identifier, preference value, partner reference, or product data.
- A7 writes Customer, `CustomerWallet`, eligibility, restrictions, limits, enrollment, permissions, risk, compliance, funding-instrument ownership, beneficiary ownership, A3 binding, A4 policy/source, or `CustomerPreference` records to make a product pass.
- A7 calls a bank/NIBSS/provider, an SMS provider, an email provider, a push provider, or any external channel from Customer, Wallet, Ledger, A5, A6, Reconciliation, diagnostics, readiness, support, or an unapproved controller instead of the isolated A6 partner boundary or the A7 notification dispatcher.
- A7 converts a notification delivery into a financial command, an A2 authorization, an A3 binding repair, an A4 policy decision, or a Ledger record.
- A7 mutates completed A5 transfer/deposit/withdrawal history, A6 settlement history, or Ledger history to reconcile a product, partner, or notification discrepancy.
- A7 begins A8, public APIs, mobile/web channels, marketing consent, broad customer activation, product expansion beyond the first selected product, or service extraction.

### 7.5 Stop conditions

A7 implementation and any later controlled product evaluation must stop when:

- The selected first product, partner, currency, capability, or product interface is not explicit and versioned.
- A2 authorization, protected ingress, service audience, privileged approval, or security context is missing, denied, stale, or mis-scoped for any A7 surface.
- A4 policy is missing, expired, pending review, denied, suspended, superseded, integrity-mismatched, or unavailable for the first product.
- A3 internal binding/account ownership, `WalletAccount`, `LedgerAccount`, currency, accounting-unit, or control state is missing, stale, repair-required, closed, suspended, incompatible, or unresolved by reconciliation.
- The external target or funding instrument is not explicitly owned, verified, current, purpose-compatible, consented, or mapped to the selected partner and the A6T04 consumer.
- Provider credentials, signing/authentication, callback authenticity, environment, endpoint, schema, or key-rotation evidence cannot be established safely through the A6 boundary.
- Internal or provider idempotency, request hash, external-operation identity, product-operation identity, provider reference, or correlation evidence is missing or conflicting.
- A provider response/callback/report cannot be authenticated, mapped, verified, or independently reconciled for the first product.
- A timeout, outage, callback replay, status-query failure, or ambiguous result cannot be placed in a durable pending/recovery/manual-review/reconciliation state.
- Settlement/suspense account configuration, Ledger invariants, currency/accounting-unit checks, or financial correction ownership is unavailable for the first product.
- Audit, outbox, idempotency, metrics, diagnostics, readiness, retention, or support evidence cannot be persisted or safely exposed.
- Reconciliation reports a missing, duplicate, orphan, amount/currency/account mismatch, unresolved suspense, stale provider report, or unexplained external outcome for the first product.
- A proposed change would edit posted Ledger history, completed A5 history, completed A6 history, Customer/A3 source records, `CustomerPreference` records, `virtual-account` records, or provider evidence to make a flow pass.
- A proposed change introduces a second product, a second partner, a new direction, FX, fees, notifications, public APIs, or A7T02+ implementation before A7T01 is approved.
- A7 admission, notification, or product disable is required but not honored.

The safe response is to deny or hold new product operations, preserve the available evidence, disable/circuit-break the selected capability, and escalate to the owning boundary. It is never to weaken a gate, infer an identity, retry an ambiguous outcome with a new key, edit source history, or begin A7T02+ work.
