# Data-Handling Decision Matrix

- **Task:** A1T12 — Draft ADR-0024 Data Classification and Privacy
- **Status:** Proposed A1 decision input; not a runtime data-governance implementation
- **Purpose:** Field/category handling, minimization, retention ownership, legal holds, and sharing decisions
- **Application code, API, entity, migration, and configuration changes:** None
- **Decision record:** [`ADR-0024-Customer-Data-Classification-Retention-and-Privacy.md`](ADR/ADR-0024-Customer-Data-Classification-Retention-and-Privacy.md)
- **Source control package:** [`IDENTIFIER-PRIVACY-RETENTION-CONTROLS.md`](IDENTIFIER-PRIVACY-RETENTION-CONTROLS.md)

## 1. Classification levels

| Level             | Meaning                                                                                              | Minimum control                                                                                      |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Public            | Approved content with no customer, credential, transaction, or security detail                       | Publication and content approval                                                                     |
| Internal          | Non-public technical, operational, or aggregate information with low standalone sensitivity          | Staff/service access; no public exposure by default                                                  |
| Confidential      | Linkable or operationally sensitive information                                                      | Least privilege, minimized logging/events, no public sharing                                         |
| Restricted        | Direct/indirect customer, identity, beneficiary, funding, financial, or security information         | Purpose/role access, encryption, masking, controlled exports, access audit                           |
| Highly Restricted | Credentials, identity evidence, investigative content, risk reasoning, or financial-control evidence | Dedicated owner, strongest access control, no plaintext/external disclosure by default, hold support |

A stricter legal, regulatory, contractual, payment-scheme, or security requirement overrides this engineering level.

## 3. Retention decision matrix

The current operational defaults come from [`RETENTION-POLICY.md`](RETENTION-POLICY.md). “Schedule required” means that the accountable owner must approve the dataset-specific schedule before deletion or external processing.

| Dataset                                                                                                                                      | Retention owner                                                        | Current baseline                                | Cleanup rule                                                                                   | Hold behavior                                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Operational metrics                                                                                                                          | Operations / Maturity                                                  | 30 days default (`METRICS_RETENTION_SECONDS`)   | Bounded aggregate cleanup                                                                      | Preserve approved incident/release evidence snapshot                |
| Audit events                                                                                                                                 | Operations with source/legal owners                                    | 365 days default (`AUDIT_RETENTION_SECONDS`)    | Controlled maintenance only; ordinary updates/deletes remain blocked                           | Hold by audit/entity/customer/case/correlation scope                |
| Idempotency records                                                                                                                          | Operations / command owner                                             | 1 day default (`IDEMPOTENCY_RETENTION_SECONDS`) | Delete expired records only; source command/resource has its own schedule                      | Preserve incident, dispute, fraud, or legal evidence                |
| Published/failed outbox events                                                                                                               | Operations / event owner                                               | 30 days default (`OUTBOX_RETENTION_SECONDS`)    | Cleanup after cutoff; payload immutable                                                        | Pending outbox is never ordinary-deleted; held events are preserved |
| Customer, identity, KYC, onboarding, eligibility, risk, compliance, credential, device, beneficiary, funding, preference, and wallet records | Respective source owner with Compliance/Security/Finance as applicable | Schedule required                               | Purpose-limited retention and approved soft-delete/anonymization; no generic cleanup           | Hold overrides all ordinary deletion/anonymization                  |
| Ledger, financial lifecycle, payment, reconciliation, and control records                                                                    | Ledger/Finance and respective financial owner                          | Financial schedule required                     | Preserve immutable financial truth and required evidence; corrections are compensating records | Hold by journal/payment/customer/dispute/incident scope             |
| Future external provider payloads and mappings                                                                                               | A6/Finance/Compliance                                                  | Schedule required before integration            | Provider contract defines minimization, retention, deletion, and evidence preservation         | Hold provider and mapped internal records together                  |

## 4. Sharing decision matrix

| Audience                         | Permitted minimum data                                                                                  | Prohibited by default                                                                                     | Required gate                                                               |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Authenticated customer           | Own masked reference/status and minimum customer/transaction display data                               | Credentials, risk factors, compliance details, internal ledger IDs, other customers, raw provider values  | A2 authentication/authorization and purpose review                          |
| Support/operations               | Minimum fields for assigned task, masked references, approved payment/correlation lookup                | Raw credentials/hashes, full identity documents, unrestricted case comments, full ledger tables           | A2 role/least privilege, access audit, support purpose                      |
| Risk/Compliance/Security         | Case/assessment/security fields within assigned scope                                                   | Unrelated customers, reusable credentials, copied source data in lower-class systems                      | Role, case scope, access logging, hold review                               |
| External KYC/bank/NIBSS/provider | Approved mapped provider ID and minimum identity/account/transaction fields                             | Internal IDs as provider identity, raw secrets/device data, unrelated risk/compliance history, full audit | A6 adapter, legal/privacy review, contract, encryption, callback validation |
| Settlement/financial partner     | Payment/provider reference, amount, currency, account fields required for settlement and reconciliation | Credentials, case comments, policy reasoning, unrestricted profile or ledger tables                       | A6/Finance approval, field mapping, reconciliation, retention terms         |
| Notification/messaging provider  | Minimum template variables, masked values, approved channel destination                                 | Credentials, security events, full financial details, identity documents, risk/compliance content         | A7 later boundary plus A2/A6 privacy controls                               |
| Analytics/observability          | Aggregates, redacted errors, purpose-specific pseudonymous IDs                                          | Raw PII, account numbers, hashes, full events, case comments, idempotency keys                            | Data owner approval, minimization, retention schedule                       |

## 5. Hold and data-subject decision rules

- A hold record names owner, reason/authority, scope, start/review/release dates, affected datasets, and release authority.
- Hold scope may use Customer UUID, case number, record UUID, payment reference, provider reference, or correlation ID and must expand through approved ownership/correlation mappings.
- Hold release is explicit and audited; cleanup re-evaluates the ordinary cutoff after release.
- Data-subject requests require authenticated access, canonical identifier lookup, purpose/legal review, hold check, and an auditable outcome. Immutable ledger, required audit, compliance, security, and held evidence are not erased by a generic customer endpoint.
- Minimization is applied at collection, mutation, audit/event construction, projection, export, partner sharing, and cleanup.

## 6. Matrix review checklist

- [ ] Every required sensitive category has an authority, steward, classification, minimization rule, retention owner, and sharing boundary.
- [ ] Credential and recovery hashes, device identifiers, and security metadata are marked Highly Restricted.
- [ ] Financial source truth is distinguished from wallet/customer metadata and operational projections.
- [ ] Audit retention is distinct from active-record and financial-record retention.
- [ ] Pending outbox events and held records cannot be removed by ordinary cleanup.
- [ ] A2 access/privilege questions and A6 external-processing questions are assigned.
- [ ] Legal, regulatory, contractual, payment-scheme, and jurisdiction questions remain explicit rather than guessed.
- [ ] No application code, schema, API, migration, or runtime behavior is introduced by this matrix.
