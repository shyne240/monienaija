## Ownership and authorization acceptance

The independent real-PostgreSQL/Fastify suite exercises CUSTOMER, AGENT and registered SYSTEM resolution; server-validated evidence; changed payload refusal; evidence revalidation inside the transaction; approval consumption and wallet mutation rollback together; concurrent competing approvals; same-approval concurrent replay; canonical UUID uniqueness including direct inserts; collision rejection; forbidden direct ownership/ledger mutations; retained wallet ID, ledger account, balance and journal history; reconciliation visibility; and continuing REVIEW/SYSTEM financial restrictions.

The registry specifies FINANCE_PREPARER as maker and an independent FINANCE_CONTROLLER as checker, with MFA, one approval and self-approval prohibition. Approval handling checks the maker's identity before role/scope approval, and execution separately checks requester identity even for `privileged:execute`. A2 workforce session creation requires MFA, and the service independently rejects non-MFA or read-only executors. Existing maker/checker tests also exercise denial over HTTP. Registry function-code arrays are empty: **dedicated assignable authorization functions remain a catalogue-owner dependency**; the existing role and `privileged:approve` fallback is not a redesigned catalogue.

An approving FINANCE_CONTROLLER may execute; an independently authorized, MFA, non-read-only `privileged:execute` principal may also execute an already-approved request. This can include a SUPER_ADMIN if granted that scope. The requester is prohibited even if they hold it. Restricting execution exclusively to the approver is an outstanding policy decision, not implemented here. There is no Admin Web ownership-resolution surface in this mission.

Additional unit coverage proves that the maker cannot self-approve even with `privileged:approve`, and cannot execute even with `privileged:execute`; neither approval consumption nor wallet mutation occurs in the latter test.

After CUSTOMER resolution, a dedicated test first demonstrates denial without a verified primary contact, then provisions controlled test-only governed evidence, a policy and assessment and observes success through the real wallet boundary; revoking the current assessment denies the same wallet. The production trusted evidence reader remains unavailable, and test-only evidence is not production verification.
