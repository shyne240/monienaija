=== ARENA 3B Stage 3 — Cash-to-Cash institutional liability integration finding ===
Finding (classification C - genuine architectural conflict):
  The pre-institutional implementation credited ONE platform-wide unclaimed account
  (CASH_TO_CASH-UNCLAIMED-NGN, institutional_scope=UNATTRIBUTED). Once the funding wallet is
  institution-attributed, LedgerService.resolveJournalInstitution correctly refuses the journal
  (it would mix an institution account with an unattributed one). Cash-to-Cash could therefore
  never post institutionally.
Resolution (no new financial policy invented):
  - src/agent/cash-to-cash-institution-suspense.ts: the obligation is the funding institution's
    own SUSPENSE liability (C2C-UNCLAIMED-*), opened through the existing governed
    institution-ledger path. Missing => 503, never a fallback to the platform account.
  - Historical transfers with no funding evidence keep the legacy account they were posted to;
    they are not re-attributed and no institution is inferred.
  - Reconciliation read models accept both the institution suspense and the legacy account.
  - Claim authorization validates the obligation account derived from the transfer itself.
