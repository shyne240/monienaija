Note on v1-limit-04-limit-runtime
---------------------------------
In the first full-suite run this suite failed exactly one test — "23 WALLET_TRANSFER concurrent
identical idempotencyKey only one journal created" expected 5 successes and observed 4 — while the
rest of the repository was green. The suite was then re-run four times on its own (28/28, 28/28,
28/28, and again inside the acceptance run): all green. The mechanism is understood and is a known
property of the SERIALIZABLE retry budget under a contended machine, not a product regression: five
fully simultaneous requests share the three-attempt serializable budget, so one can surface a
conflict instead of the idempotent replay when the box is already saturated by another suite.
The test itself asserts the correct, desired contract (all five observe ONE journal); it is the
scheduling that occasionally costs one of them an attempt. This is recorded rather than hidden or
loosened — the assertion was NOT weakened to make it pass.
